# ECO 701: Microeconomic Theory

Visit the site this repo builds at [https://jhconning.github.io/hunter_eco701/](https://jhconning.github.io/hunter_eco701/)

**Hunter College, Department of Economics — Fall 2026**

A Master's level course in modern microeconomic theory. We study the behavior of
actors who optimize subject to constraints, derive their best response
functions, and ask whether the resulting equilibrium interactions lead to social
outcomes that are efficient or otherwise desirable.

Mondays, 5:30–7:30pm, Hunter West 113.
Instructor: [Jonathan Conning](mailto:jconning@hunter.cuny.edu).

**Slides for each lecture are posted here on the day of that lecture.** They are
revised up to the last minute, so do not expect to find them in advance.
