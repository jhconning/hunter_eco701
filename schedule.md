## Optional readings and material mentioned in lecture

Context for the curious. None of it is examinable unless separately assigned.

**Week 1.**
Hayek (1945), "The Use of Knowledge in Society," *American Economic Review*
35(4): 519–530 — [free at Econlib](https://www.econlib.org/library/Essays/hykKnw.html).
The price system as a machine for aggregating dispersed knowledge.
Jordan (2025), "A Collectivist, Economic Perspective on AI" —
[arXiv:2507.06268](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.06268). A position paper arguing
that the AI era is a distributed mechanism-design problem rather than a coming
superintelligence. Pairs with Hayek, eighty years apart.

**Week 2.**
Calvano, Calzolari, Denicolò & Pastorello (2020), "Artificial Intelligence,
Algorithmic Pricing, and Collusion," *American Economic Review* 110(10) —
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.20190623). Pricing algorithms that learn
supracompetitive prices without communicating.
Goodfellow et al. (2014), "Generative Adversarial Networks" —
[arXiv:1406.2661](https://arxiv.org/abs/1406.2661). Training one of these is a
search for the Nash equilibrium of a continuous zero-sum game.

**Week 6.**
Ouyang et al. (2022), "Training language models to follow instructions with
human feedback" — [arXiv:2203.02155](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.02155). The
reward model is a learned utility function, elicited from pairwise comparisons.

**Week 13.**
Shumailov et al. (2024), "AI models collapse when trained on recursively
generated data," *Nature* 631 — [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07566-y).
A commons problem in the shared stock of training data.

**Week 14.**
Holmström & Milgrom (1991), "Multitask Principal–Agent Analyses," *Journal of
Law, Economics, and Organization* 7 —
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/jleo/7.special_issue.24). The formal statement of
"reward the measurable and the unmeasured margin degrades."
Amodei et al. (2016), "Concrete Problems in AI Safety" —
[arXiv:1606.06565](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.06565). Reward hacking as the same
problem in another setting.
