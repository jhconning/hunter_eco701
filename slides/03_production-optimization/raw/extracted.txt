## Slide 165

- Production Technology versus Production Technique
- A Production Technology is like a recipe book.  The available recipes (techniques) for producing any amount Q of output.  
- An isoquant map summarizes the technology.  A point on an isoquant (and sometimes a K/L ratio) is a production technique.

## Slide 166

- Production Technology versus Production Technique
- A Production technique is like a recipe.  
  - How to make Q=10 widgets
    - Recipe 1: “Combine 20 units of K and 10 units of L”
    - Recipe 2: “Combine 10 units of K and 20 units of L”
    - Recipe 3: “Combine 12 units of K and 12 units of L”
  - These are “fixed coefficient” techniques.  But other “convex combination” recipes emerge. For example (assuming CRS):
    - Recipe 4: Produce Q=10 widgets with (16K, 11L) by using ½ of recipe 1 (10K,5L) and ½ of recipe 3 (6K,6L).
  - An Isoquant for Q=10 can be traced out (next slide)
