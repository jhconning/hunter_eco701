# Topic 6 Plan: General Equilibrium I & II

Unified two-lecture deck for Classes 11 (Mon Nov 23) and 12 (Mon Nov 30), Fall 2026.
Readings: Nicholson & Snyder (11th ed.) Ch. 13; Bowles & Halliday (2022) Ch. 4 (high-level) and Ch. 14.

## Structure

- **Part I (Class 11): Production, the PPF, Trade, and Factor Prices**
  1. One-Farm / Robinson Crusoe economy (planner vs. decentralized labor market, directly supporting PS3 Q4) + interactive wage slider (`widget_robinson.html`).
  2. Building the PPF, no-markets proof that $RPT = MC_x / MC_y$, closed-economy equilibrium, and autarky vs. trade + interactive PPF/trade widget (`widget_ppf_trade.html`).
  3. 2×2 Production Edgeworth Box ($RTS_x = RTS_y$), why differing factor intensities bow the PPF outward under CRS + interactive linked Cobb–Douglas Production Box ↔ PPF (`widget_prod_box.html`).
  4. Corn Laws (1815–1846) & Stolper–Samuelson (`fig_corn_laws.png`), political economy of trade and agrarian structure, and why the representative consumer hides distribution.

- **Part II (Class 12): Exchange, the Core, Power, Welfare Theorems, and Full GE**
  1. Exchange Edgeworth Box, Ana & Ben credit market from Topic 5 / PS3 Q2–Q3 (`fig_ana_ben_box.png`), contract curve ($MRS^A = MRS^B$), UPF & Social Welfare Functions (`fig_upf_swf.png`).
  2. Property rights, Participation Constraints (PC), the Core, and high-level BH Ch. 4 contrast: TIOLI power (PC → efficient, all rent) vs. Price-Setting / Monopoly power (ICC / offer curve → inefficient, DWL).
  3. Walrasian equilibrium, offer curves, short-side rationing + interactive Exchange Box (`widget_exchange_box.html`).
  4. $n$-good exchange, Walras' Law, Brouwer existence intuition, First Welfare Theorem (NS proof + BH perfect-competition vs. complete-contracts decomposition + Hayek 1945).
  5. Distributional neutrality, Second Welfare Theorem (land reform vs. price controls; enclosures), SMD uniqueness caveat & Foley–Smale disequilibrium path dependence (closing Topic 1's Schelling loop).
  6. Integrating production and consumption: nested Exchange Box inside PPF (`fig_nested_ppf_box.png`), Walras' Law with profit shares, GE tax incidence, and handoff to Class 13 (externalities, commons, Coase).
