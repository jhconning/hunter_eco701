# Narration script — Episode 1: The Cobb-Douglas Production Function

Written to `..\narration_rubric.md` (series-root rubric — applies to every
episode). Conventions:

- **One assertion per line.** A blank line is a breath; `[beat]` is a short
  deliberate pause.
- **`[SILENCE — …, ~Ns]` is a hard direction:** say nothing; the animation
  is carrying the argument.
- `(hold ~Ns)` marks how much room the cut gives the lines that follow —
  budget ≈ 2.4 words per second. Every beat below fits the current render;
  if something feels rushed read aloud, stretch that scene's `wait()`s and
  re-render — never read faster.
- Bold-face words are the ONE stress point of their sentence.
- Scene start times match `renders\episode1_cobb_douglas_1080p30.mp4`.

## S2 · The expression — 1:21 (80 s)

[ON SCREEN: q = A K^α L^β writes large, colored]

The **formula** lens first.

[legend rows fade in one at a time — ~3s each]

q: output.

A: total factor productivity — the technology knob.

K and L: the inputs. Capital, labor.

Alpha and beta: the output elasticities. Keep an eye on these.

(hold ~5s)
One computation now — the payoff lands at the end of the video.

[scaling algebra animates, line by line]

Scale **both** inputs by t.

[beat]

The t's collect.

[box surrounds the result; "homogeneous of degree α+β" — hold ~9s]

Output scales by t to the alpha-plus-beta.

That's homogeneity —
and the degree is the whole story of scale.

[number line, three labeled zones — hold ~10s]

Below one: doubling inputs less than doubles output.

Above one: more than doubles.

Exactly one — constant returns to scale.

[A=1, α=β=½ appears; q = √(KL) transforms in — hold ~3s]

Our example sits right there.

A is one. Alpha and beta: one-half each.

Output is the square root of K times L.

[numeric check line — hold ~6s]

One and four make two.

Two and eight make four.

Double in — double out.

[running-example badge appears — hold ~8s]

Now let's watch this formula become a **shape**.

## S5 · MRTS — 4:32 (79 s)

[ON SCREEN: the map; three curves dim, q̄ = 4 stays bright]

Settle in on one curve.

[derivation panel, line by line — holds ~5s, ~6s]

Before any calculus, the intuition.

Staying on the curve means output doesn't move.

So whatever extra output the new labor brings,
the capital you give up must take away — **exactly**.

[the total differential writes]

That sentence, written down, is this equation.

Gains cancel losses.

[slope line writes — hold ~6s]

Solve it:
the slope of an isoquant is a ratio of marginal products.

[MRTS definition card — hold ~9s]

The formal name, once:

the marginal rate of technical substitution —
minus dK by dL, output held at q-bar —
equals F-L over F-K.

Capital released, per extra unit of labor.

Nicholson says RTS. The consumer twin is the MRS.

[Cobb-Douglas partials appear — hold ~6s]

For Cobb-Douglas, the ratio collapses.

[boxed result — hold ~7s]

Beta over alpha, times K over L.

Alpha equals beta here — so, simply: K over L.

[dot, tangent, and readout appear on the curve]

Watch the readout as we ride down.

[SILENCE — the slide; readout falls 4.00 → 0.25, ~7s]

Four units of capital per worker, up top.

A quarter, down here.

[caption: diminishing MRTS — hold ~10s]

The more labor you already have,
the less capital each extra worker replaces.

Diminishing MRTS.

That's why isoquants bow toward the origin.

## S6 · Homotheticity — 5:51 (82 s)

[ON SCREEN: full family; a ray shoots from the origin]

Remember the homogeneity computation?

Payoff time.

A ray from the origin is a fixed **recipe** —
a constant ratio of capital to labor.

[red tangent stubs appear at every crossing — hold ~3s]

Mark the slope at every crossing.

(hold ~5s)
Parallel.

Every single one.

[MRTS formula, right panel — hold ~6s]

No accident.

MRTS is beta over alpha, times K over L.

It depends on the **ratio** alone.

[general proof line, bottom — hold ~9s]

And it's general. Three rungs:

F: homogeneous of degree r.

Its partials: degree r minus one.

Their ratio: degree **zero**. Scale-proof.

[homothetic definition card — hold ~8s]

Formally, once:

a homothetic technology holds its MRTS constant
along every ray from the origin.

[SILENCE — the ray sweeps steep, shallow, back; stubs stay parallel, ~12s]

Any recipe.

Same story.

[payoff lines — hold ~10s]

Translate.

The algebra: a degree-zero ratio.

The picture: one curve, rescaled.

The economics: a bigger firm is a scaled **copy** —
same recipe, more of it.

[tease line — hold ~8s]

Expansion paths are straight rays.

Next time: prices land on this map,
and Lagrange finds the best point on every curve.
